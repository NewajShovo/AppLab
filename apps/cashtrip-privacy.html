Privacy Policy

Last updated October 13, 2026.

This policy describes how CashTrip: Travel Money Tracker ("CashTrip") handles information.

Information we collect

The developer of CashTrip does not collect any personal information through the app. The app does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, or location. The amounts you convert are not sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Information kept on your device

Settings such as your favorite currencies and recent conversions are stored only on your iPhone so the app can remember them between uses. They are never uploaded, and deleting the app removes them.

Exchange rates

To show up-to-date rates, CashTrip downloads exchange rate data from a third-party rate provider. These requests do not include any personal information or the amounts you enter. As with any internet request, the provider may receive basic technical information, such as your IP address, in order to respond.

Ads and analytics

CashTrip does not show ads, does not use third-party analytics, and does not track you across other apps or websites. If that changes in a future version, this page will be updated first.

Your consent

By using CashTrip, you consent to this policy.

Children's privacy

CashTrip is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change too. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the support page.
