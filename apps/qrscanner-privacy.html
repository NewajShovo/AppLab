QR Scanner / Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026.

This policy describes how QR Scanner handles information.

Information we collect

The developer of QR Scanner does not collect any personal information through the app. The app does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, or location.

Information kept on your device

Your scan history is stored only on your iPhone so you can find codes again later. It is never uploaded, and you can clear it in the app or by deleting the app.

Camera

QR Scanner asks for camera access so it can read codes. The camera feed is processed on your device to find and decode QR codes. No photos or video are saved or sent to the developer.

Links and other content

When you choose to open a link from a scanned code, it opens in your browser or another app. Those websites and apps have their own privacy policies, which the developer does not control.

Ads and analytics

QR Scanner does not show ads, does not use third-party analytics, and does not track you across other apps or websites. If that changes in a future version, this page will be updated first.

Your consent

By using QR Scanner, you consent to this policy.

Children's privacy

QR Scanner is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change too. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the support page.
