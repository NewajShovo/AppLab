Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026.

This policy describes how Renewa handles information.

Information we collect

The developer of Renewa does not collect any personal information through the app. The app does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, or location.

Information kept on your device

The subscriptions, prices, and renewal dates you enter are stored only on your iPhone so the app can show them between uses. Renewa does not connect to your bank or read your purchases. Your entries are never uploaded, and deleting the app removes them.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Renewa uses them to remind you before a renewal. These reminders are scheduled on your device and are not sent through the developer's servers.

Ads and analytics

Renewa does not show ads, does not use third-party analytics, and does not track you across other apps or websites. If that changes in a future version, this page will be updated first.

Your consent

By using Renewa, you consent to this policy.

Children's privacy

Renewa is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change too. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the support page.
