Luna Cycle / Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026.

This policy describes how Luna Cycle handles information.

Information we collect

The developer of Luna Cycle does not collect any personal information through the app. The app does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, or location.

Information kept on your device

Everything you log, including period dates, symptoms, and notes, is stored only on your iPhone. It is never uploaded to the developer, never sold, and never shared with advertisers or data brokers. Deleting the app removes it.

Sensitive health information

Cycle and symptom data is sensitive. Because it stays on your device, the developer cannot see, access, or hand it over to anyone. Protect it by keeping a passcode on your iPhone.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Luna Cycle uses them to remind you about an upcoming period. These reminders are scheduled on your device and are not sent through the developer's servers.

Ads and analytics

Luna Cycle does not show ads, does not use third-party analytics, and does not track you across other apps or websites. If that changes in a future version, this page will be updated first.

Your consent

By using Luna Cycle, you consent to this policy.

Children's privacy

Luna Cycle is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change too. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the support page.
