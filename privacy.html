Privacy, plainly stated.

Last updated September 24, 2026.

AppLab is designed to be useful without collecting more information than we need. This starter policy explains how this website and our small apps handle information.

Information we collect

The website does not require an account and does not ask you to submit personal information. The currency converter runs locally in your browser and does not send the values you enter to a server.

Analytics and cookies

This starter site does not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics by default. If we add measurement tools later, this policy will be updated before they are used.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the AppLab team through the details on our support page.
