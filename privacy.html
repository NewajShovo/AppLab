Privacy, plainly stated.

Last updated September 24, 2026.

AppLab is designed to be useful without collecting more information than we need. This starter policy explains how this website and our small apps handle information.

Information we collect

The website does not require an account and does not ask you to submit personal information, such as your name, email address, or location. Ads and analytics

This starter site does not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics by default. If we add measurement or advertising tools later, this policy will be updated before they are used.

Your consent

By using an AppLab website or app, you consent to this privacy policy.

Contact
